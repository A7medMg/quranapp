# Hadith data sources

## Sahih Muslim

- Edition: `ara-muslim` from [fawazahmed0/hadith-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api)
- Download endpoint: `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/ara-muslim.min.json`
- Bundled Arabic entries: 7,360. Entries without Arabic text are omitted.

## Sahih al-Bukhari

- Edition: `ara-bukhari` from [fawazahmed0/hadith-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api)
- Download endpoint: `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/ara-bukhari.min.json`
- Bundled Arabic entries: 7,580. Entries without Arabic text are omitted.

The upstream repository identifies its data as Unlicense. Arabic text is bundled as supplied by the edition; the app does not edit or grade individual narrations. The collection's own number and the source edition number are both retained where available.
